# Growth metrics

North star: weekly active profit-tracked agents—unique agents or permitted
hashed payer identities completing at least two valid calculations containing at
least ten accepted economic events within seven days.

Thirty-day targets: 30 qualified prospects; 30 approved personalized contacts;
12 replies; 8 integration conversations; 5 test integrations; 5 first paid
calculations; 3 repeat agents; 2 workspaces; 1 attestation; 2 case studies.

Report actual zeros and unknowns. Never create synthetic calls to improve npm,
registry, marketplace, or payment metrics.
