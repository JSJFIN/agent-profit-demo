# FAQ

**Is wallet balance profit?** No. Capital, withdrawals, transfers, asset events,
and timing differences change cash without the same profit treatment.

**Does an LLM calculate profit?** No. Decimal arithmetic and versioned rules do.

**Is evidence verified?** Only to the explicit evidence level. Self-reported
events remain self-reported.

**Are stateless events stored?** Raw stateless batches are not retained. A paid
workspace retains events for its configured period, normally 30 days.

**What does signing prove?** Content integrity and the service signing key. It
does not audit the underlying events.

**Is this accounting or tax advice?** No. It is operational profit analytics.

**How can an agent integrate?** MCP, the npm SDK, OpenAPI, or direct x402 HTTP.
