# Design-partner questionnaire

- What paid agent, API, or MCP operation are you testing?
- Which currencies and networks produce revenue or costs?
- What are the revenue, refund, model, API, hosting, acquisition, and fee sources?
- Which events have stable external IDs and source names?
- Which agent, venture, product, customer, or experiment identifiers are useful?
- Which evidence can be self-reported, receipt-supplied, or verified?
- Do you need stateless processing or a 30-day workspace?
- What result would make the integration operationally useful?
- May we measure anonymous funnel milestones? (optional)
- May any aggregated result become a case study? (separate optional consent)
- What integration friction or missing documentation did you encounter?
