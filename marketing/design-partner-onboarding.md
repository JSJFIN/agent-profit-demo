# Design-partner onboarding

1. Confirm the partner has real economic events and a public/authorized contact.
2. Choose stateless or a 30-day workspace and begin on Base Sepolia.
3. Map revenue, refunds, model/API, hosting, acquisition, x402, and gas events.
4. Add at least one venture or experiment identifier and explicit evidence levels.
5. Validate locally, request a quote, and agree an explicit payment ceiling.
6. Run the first calculation; reconcile accepted, rejected, duplicate, and
   profit-excluded counts.
7. Review the first-week report and data-quality warnings with the partner.
8. Ask separately for any public case-study permission. Default is private.

Delete or let workspace data expire when the test ends. Never collect wallet
keys, recovery phrases, raw payment authorizations, or unnecessary personal data.
