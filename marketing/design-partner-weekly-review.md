# Weekly partner review

Partner / week:

- Events submitted, accepted, rejected, and duplicated
- Profit-affecting versus profit-excluded events
- Revenue, refunds, costs, net profit, and net cash flow by currency
- Venture and experiment contribution
- Unattributed costs and missing categories
- Evidence coverage and unsupported claims
- First calculation / second calculation / workspace / attestation milestone
- Integration time and blockers
- Data scheduled for deletion or expiry
- Partner feedback and one agreed next step
- Case-study permission: not asked / declined / private / written approval
