# 60–90 second demonstration

Headline: **This AI agent's wallet gained 570 USDC. It only made 70 USDC
profit.** All data is synthetic.

1. Install/discover (10 seconds): show `npm install @jsjfin/agent-profit` and
   `agent-profit doctor --profile testnet`, or MCP tool discovery.
2. Events (15 seconds): show 100 USDC customer revenue, 30 USDC LLM expense, and
   500 USDC owner capital in `examples/profit-loop-events.json`.
3. Quote (10 seconds): run `agent-profit quote calculate
examples/profit-loop-events.json --profile testnet`. Point to “Payment will
   not be authorized.”
4. Result (15 seconds): show net operating profit 70 USDC and cash increase 570
   USDC from a previously verified fixture or an explicitly authorized testnet
   call.
5. Explain (15 seconds): `100 revenue − 30 operating cost = 70 profit`; owner
   capital increased cash but was not revenue.
6. Close (10 seconds): invite five design partners to map seven days of agent
   revenue and costs with hands-on help.

Shot list: terminal install; three-row JSON; decoded x402 quote; result fields;
capital-versus-profit diagram; MCP/npm/OpenAPI/x402 links. Do not imply the
synthetic amounts are customer activity.
