# Five-partner program

Offer: free integration assistance, Base Sepolia testing, event mapping, a
first-week profit report, a direct feedback channel, and an optional anonymized
case study.

Partner contribution: at least seven days of economic events; revenue and cost
categories; one venture or experiment identifier; candid integration feedback;
permission to measure an anonymous funnel; optional written permission for any
public result.

Success criteria: five integrations started, five first valid calculations,
three second calculations, two workspaces, one signed attestation, and two
publishable case studies.

No profitability, revenue improvement, audit, or business outcome is promised.
