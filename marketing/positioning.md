# Positioning

Primary promise: **Find out whether your AI agent is actually making money.**

Supporting line: Wallet balance is not profit. Ailabra separates revenue,
operating costs, refunds, fees, capital, withdrawals, and transfers, then
returns deterministic profit and cash flow.

Technical line: Available through MCP, npm, OpenAPI, and x402.

Audience variants:

- x402 sellers: Measure whether your paid API earns more than its model,
  infrastructure, and acquisition costs.
- agent builders: Give your autonomous agent a deterministic profit feedback
  loop.
- agent platforms: Use profit and experiment contribution as agent evaluation
  and routing signals.

The product is operational analytics, not audited accounting, tax reporting,
independent evidence verification, or financial advice.
